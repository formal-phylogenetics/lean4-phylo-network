# Phylogenetic network estimation, in Lean

[![CI](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-network/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-network/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Lean 4 formalizations of papers on estimating phylogenetic networks, rooted and semi-directed, from
gene trees, small subnetworks, several trees or sequences.

This repository is a hub of inference in the collection rooted at
[lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics). It holds the
library [`PhyloNetwork`](PhyloNetwork), for what two or more of its papers share, and the catalog of
the papers, [`catalog/papers.toml`](catalog/papers.toml), from
which the status and the checklist below are generated. The library has hypothesis tests on quartet
concordance factors (Mitchell, Allman, Rhodes 2019), the blobs, wedge paths and blocks of level-1
networks, cut splits of induced networks, the chain of the network multispecies coalescent on a
concrete network, and the results of Rhodes 2020 and Huber et al. 2025 that several papers cite; its
[README](PhyloNetwork/README.md) lists the modules. What only the papers on the tree of blobs, a
step of network estimation, share is in the library `TreeOfBlobs` of
[lean4-phylo-tree-of-blobs](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-tree-of-blobs), which
they require besides this one.

The collection's
[conventions](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md) and
[workflow](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/WORKFLOW.md)
are in the root. Each paper is formalized with the
[formalize-math-paper](https://github.com/vltanh/formalize-math-paper) skill: its results are proved
by the paper's own arguments, and an audit reports its errors and gaps.

## The papers

Each paper is in the family of its method, with the theory of that method; methods that stop at the
tree of blobs are in the family Tree of blobs. Methods that stop at a species tree are in
[lean4-phylo-species-tree](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylo-species-tree).

## Starting a paper

From [lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/formal-phylogenetics/lean4-phylogenetics), when the
paper's formalization starts:

```sh
python3 scripts/status.py next             # the open papers, by priority
python3 scripts/new_paper.py <id>          # creates ../papers/network/lean4-<id>
cd ../papers/network/lean4-<id> && claude  # then: /formalize-math-paper <the paper's link>
```

## Building

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake build Phylogenetics
lake env lean scripts/Audit.lean
```

Lean and Mathlib `v4.37.0-rc1`. [`lakefile.toml`](lakefile.toml) requires lean4-phylogenetics at the
commits of the train. The audit, [`scripts/Audit.lean`](scripts/Audit.lean), checks that every
declaration of this library and of the libraries it requires uses only Lean's standard axioms;
`python3 scripts/gen_audit.py` writes its `import all` lines. CI builds the library, runs the audit,
and checks that the status and the checklist above are current.
